## Composition and hierarchy

1. **Layered backgrounds instead of flat solids** *(shared)* — Flat solids are fine because they do not distract, but if every background is flat the piece lacks identity; Vox uses image backgrounds constantly.
   - use solids sparingly for focus beats
   - default to a layered image background
   - Why: identity and editorial feel
   - Build: background layer built from images, not fillRect
   - Source: reel `DY7TSLJAQef` 0:10-0:22
