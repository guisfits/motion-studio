# Zhylin: Vox lessons for the storyboard

Source: Denys Zhylin, tutorial notes in tutorials/zhylin-five-vox-tricks.md (timestamps as given).

1. Every chapter opens with the same card (1.5-2.5 s): numeral, then the chapter name building one word per beat, ~0.5 s apart (4-5.5 s, 15-18 s, 26-28 s, 40-42 s). Plan the card as one reusable asset in the manifest, not one per chapter (takeaway 2).
2. Every cut shows ONE subject for ONE noun; no shot is decorative (takeaway 3).
3. Fast montage only under a list sentence: one shot per noun, ~0.7-1 s each (5.5-8 s, 18-21 s), ~0.5 s per item when the items are map regions (35-39.5 s). Bursts at 9.1-9.5, 12-14.3, 45.5-47.9 s sit under one sentence (takeaway 4).
4. An explanatory sentence gets the longest picture of the film: one subject held 3.1 s (14.8-18 s) to 5.5 s (27.9-33.4 s), with a label on it and a second event in it (a highlight, a slow push) (takeaway 4).
5. A number gets its own big shot with the figure and its label (5.5-8 s; "55 million", 35-39.5 s); the counter rule is the animator's.
6. Return to a talking-head or other anchor shot every ~25-30 s (0, 34, 58 s) to re-anchor after dense picture runs ~ (takeaway 6; only when the film has an on-camera anchor).
