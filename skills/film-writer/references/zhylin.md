# Zhylin: Vox lessons for the writer

Source: Denys Zhylin, tutorial notes in tutorials/zhylin-five-vox-tricks.md (timestamps as given).

1. Promise N, deliver N: the opening names a count ("five tricks", 0-1.5 s) and the film delivers exactly that many chapters, in order (structure, 62.5 s reel).
2. Each chapter runs claim, proof, one sentence of "why": the name, then examples, then a single line that says what the examples add up to (structure, every chapter).
3. The last item reframes the list instead of adding one more: "it's not only X, it's how they put everything together" (47.5-56 s; takeaway 7).
4. A list line is written as nouns the picture can cut on ("Numbers. Places. Historical events.", 5.5-8 s; "Maps. Newspapers. Diagrams.", 18-21 s): one short noun per ~0.7-1 s.
5. An explanatory line is a full sentence that can carry a long hold (28-33.4 s, 5.5 s); keep list lines and explanatory lines apart, not mixed in one breath (takeaway 4).
6. Narration is continuous: no silence over ~0.4 s from 0 to 62 s (silencedetect found none); pauses are cut in the take, not left as air (Measurements).
