# Zhylin: Vox lessons for the art-director

Source: Denys Zhylin, tutorial notes in tutorials/zhylin-five-vox-tricks.md (timestamps as given).

1. One chapter-card template for the whole film: same ground, same large numeral, same corner mark, same title voice; only the numeral and the title change (takeaway 2; 4-5.5, 15-18, 26-28, 40-42, 47.5-50.5 s). The numeral and the title use two distinct type voices (a large serif numeral over an italic serif title in the source).
2. A faint engraved image on the card ground gives texture without competing with the numeral (card, 4-5.5 s) ~.
3. Captions stay burned in at one fixed spot in the lower third, bold sans, 2-4 words at a time (Measurements, takeaway 1); a key word may take a plate (`playbook.md` §4 and `NORMS.caption` govern size, contrast and zone; the register-A caption is a small serif, grammar §12.7).
4. Cut-out subjects sit on a plain grid ground with a one-word label ("PRESIDENT", 28-33.4 s); grid, label and cut-out stay in one consistent set of treatments.
