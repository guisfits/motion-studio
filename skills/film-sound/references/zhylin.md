# Zhylin: Vox lessons for the sound

Source: Denys Zhylin, tutorial notes in tutorials/zhylin-five-vox-tricks.md (timestamps as given).

1. The voice is constant and loud, flat in dynamics: integrated -14.1 LUFS, LRA 1.5 LU (Measurements); this agrees with `NORMS.audio`.
2. SFX are rare, ~1 per 6 s (~10 in 62.5 s), and sit only on a number card or a transition, never on plain talk (Measurements; takeaway 5). Level traces only, the source could not listen (~).
3. SFX never mask the voice: RMS stays steady at -16..-17 dBFS throughout (Measurements).
