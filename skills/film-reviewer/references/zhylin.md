# Zhylin: Vox lessons for the reviewer

Source: Denys Zhylin, tutorial notes in tutorials/zhylin-five-vox-tricks.md (timestamps as given).

1. Tell: a shot with no noun in the line it sits under, or one that only fills time (takeaway 3). Log its `t`.
2. Tell: a montage burst under an explanatory sentence, or a long hold under a list sentence (takeaway 4). Fast under lists, slow under reasoning.
3. Tell: chapter cards that differ in ground, numeral, corner mark or title voice across chapters (takeaway 2).
4. Tell: the promised count does not match the delivered count (0-1.5 s versus the chapters).
5. Tell: the last chapter only adds an item and does not reframe the list (takeaway 7).
6. Tell: SFX spread evenly across plain talk instead of landing on cards and transitions (Measurements, ~1 per 6 s).
