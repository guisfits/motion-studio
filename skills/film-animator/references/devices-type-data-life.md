# Devices: captions, lettering, type, life, data

Device -> when -> how in this engine -> measured range -> tell and fix. `grammar §n` = `research/editing-grammar.md`; `craft §n` = `research/craft.md`; ids are pieces measured there; `moran/<reel>` = a lesson in `skills/_shared/moran.md`; `[eng]` = engine code read; `~` = inference. Gate thresholds are `NORMS` keys. Size, face, plate and colour of every kind of type are the art director's (style bible); you own timing, motion and the trace (`trace.ts`).

## 1. Captions (caption as the motion, in detail)

The register-A caption is 2-4 words of narration in a small high-contrast serif, often italic, replaced in place at a fixed position (grammar §7, §1).
- Measured A: cap height 2.5-3.5 % of frame height (`NORMS.caption.capHeight` = [0.025, 0.045]; `hex/Dbc-vIXCCqe` 3.5, `hex/Dd0qOzpqoYf` 3, `hex/Da0NQ7eCAnr` 2.5); chunks of 1-3, 2-4, 2-5 words (`hex/DU3hTiOk2-M`, `hex/DbnDiSLCtax`, `hex/DY_8VpvCpeO`); swaps in place at one position across cuts (`hex/DP6L1BqCc0g`, `hex/DbnDiSLCtax`). Caption cap height >= 4 % in craft §2.5 is superseded by the measured range (grammar §12, last paragraph).
- Chunking (yours): from `film.json` `words` (`fromWords`, `pageWindows`), in order, at most `NORMS.caption.maxWords` words, never across a sentence break, text exactly as spoken (case and accents kept). A chunk starts at its first word's start. Never one chip per word typed in as it is said, never a chunk swapped mid-phrase (postmortem C2).
- Reading time: plateau = max(`NORMS.caption.minLegibleS`, characters / `NORMS.caption.maxCps`); plan ~15 cps on a busy picture, add ~0.25 s to one- and two-word chunks, more for caps, italic and script (craft §2.5, `~`). T1 and T2 count only frames at alpha >= `NORMS.caption.legibleAlpha`.
- Timing in practice: a chunk ends at the earlier of its plateau and 0.05 s before the next chunk starts. The measured swap is a hard replacement in place, so fades are for the first chunk after a gap and the last before one (6-10 frames in, 4-6 frames out, craft §2.5); inside a run, swap with no fade, otherwise the fades eat the legible time. Worked check: a 3-word chunk spans ~1.0 s of speech at 3.0 words/s; with 0.2 s of fade-in and 0.13 s of fade-out it is legible 0.62 s and T2 fires. If the voice is too fast for the plateau, do not shrink the hold and do not re-chunk past the word limit: route the line to the writer.
- Placement: screen space above the camera (a sibling of `<Camera>`, never moved by it, never on twos, never blurred); inside the safe box (`layoutOf(format).safe`); on sky, water, wall, chest or dark mid-tones, never on faces or across eyes and mouth (`hex/DcF_qVxqsaA`, `hex/DOOCNSVCexy`); white with a soft glow on footage, black on paper (`hex/DbnDiSLCtax`). T11 reads the pixels behind the text, so the caption sits on the quiet area the art director made for it. The caption does not cover a beat's zoom target: move the target (framing) before moving the caption into the platform zones.
- Emphasis: the key word may take the style bible's single emphasis (italic in the same size, `hex/Dbc-vIXCCqe`; or the size jump, the art director's). A size jump above `NORMS.caption.capHeight` is emphasis lettering, not a caption: trace it as `lettering` and keep the caption running under it (grammar §12.5).
- Set in `RevealWords` (words arrive on their onset, `revealWord` gives the alpha the trace reads); `captionBox` and `captionItem` put the chunk in the trace, and the font size, line height and face are the same constants in both files (the self-check loop measures the ink against `box`). One id per chunk (`cap-<n>`), `text` as displayed; a reused id merges legible time across different words and hides T1 and T2.
- Tell: tiny low-contrast caption over a moving face or bright sky (grammar §11 #3). Fix: quiet area, band or glow, below the chin.
- Persistent bar variant (S8 clone of `hex/Dd0qOzpqoYf`, client 9/10): a flat accent bar at the frame centre, as wide as its whole phrase from the first frame, typing the phrase inside it (~2.5 characters per frame at 24 fps, from 1 frame after the phrase's first word), swapped in place, waiting empty for 4-6 frames on cards. Chunk by sense, not by count: at most 3 words and ~1 s of speech, never across a comma or full stop, and never ending on a function word (to, and, the, a, of, in, like, who, that, when, on, was): it moves to the next bar ("on weekends" | "to watch"). A phrase spoken while another device owned the screen (title, text wall, card) never reaches the bar: after that window the bar waits empty as wide as the next phrase. "Spoken while" is judged at the phrase's midpoint, so a phrase that starts a few frames before the cut but is said after it still gets its bar (the reference shows "Inside," from the first frame after the card). Cards may set the bar smaller and lower; that is a per-window look, not a second caption system. Mechanism: `studio/films/clone-bethlem/lib/kit.tsx` `CaptionBar`.

## 2. Draw-on markup and lettering

**Draw-on markup.** Circles, arrows, underlines, routes draw themselves on the word (grammar §5).
- Measured: 3-6 frames for a quick mark (`pin/349988`, `ee/DY9SS5YOhS8` 6 frames for a marker ellipse); 0.3-0.5 s for scribbles (`ee/DeB2BnjOvHp`); ~0.6 s arc (`hex/DapVrzSiKJR`); ~0.8 s map circle (`hex/DXoOsJdiSeU`); ~1 s loop round a figure (`hex/Dae8G-ZiP7P`); 1.2-1.5 s route (`hex/DVL9nizj2HD`, `hex/DYMUSnRiNbV`); script written in ~1.5-2 s as a second voice (`hex/DYOyiPDI666`, `hex/DYZBoYQiQBA`).
- When: pick a person out of a group photo (`hex/DdqZ3qKKe7f`); trace a route (`hex/DcS2NcaCHBM`); circle the paradox word (`hex/DNDJyk8pizL`).
- How: the text and its word come from the sheet's `lettering[]`: `text` is displayed, `says` (else `text`) is the spoken word, `lands` is that word's start; draw nothing the narrator does not say, never retype a time. The write-on starts within `NORMS.sync.letteringToleranceFrames` of `lands`, never later; for a narrated mark it runs 0.3-0.6 s (the word's own length clamped; a mark of 3-6 frames is the fast variant, a route the long one). `useDraw(landing, dur, lead, onTwos)` gives the 0..1 progress (`landing` = `{ at }` seconds or `{ word, captions }`; starts `lead` frames before the onset, exactly 1 at the end so the last glyph is never cropped), left to right, on twos by default with seeded width jitter. Components: `Underline` and `Strike` (both `Stroke`: seeded jittery passes), `Highlight` (marker sweep, `rgba(255,214,10,0.6)`, put it `mix-blend-mode: multiply`), `DrawPath` (a mark or arrow along a path), `RouteMap` (point to point, `routeHead` gives the tip for the camera to follow). `CircleMark` draws a hand-drawn ellipse round a word or box (`circleMarkPath` / `circleMarkBox` for the trace), `Handwrite` writes margin script on in `FONT_SCRIPT` (`handwriteProgress`); a page-zoom-to-quote is camera keys plus `Highlight` (`devices-transitions-entrances.md` 2).
- Hand-made, not vector: Trim Paths with a tapered end (`moran/DLShlJxRXZe`, `moran/DT2_SIMgBfI`), roughen edges and boil on 12 fps (`moran/DMixnWEpuhj`, `moran/DZdCD2YNk4d`); underline ~12-14 px at 1080 wide, three passes, rough ends; a highlighter behind the word at alpha ~0.6 (craft §2.6). A flat perfect line reads as UI.
- Pacing: one mark at a time; at most `NORMS.sync.maxMarksPerSecond` per second across a beat border (S6 on the sheet); mark only the word that carries the claim. The highlighter bar is half a line high so letters overlap it (`hex/DbXtTeqifQw`); reveals left to right in ~0.4-0.6 s.
- Retire before the camera leaves the shot, about 0.4 s before the beat ends and before the next caption lands; while traced it must not cross a frame edge (T4) (`~`: craft §2.6 asks the annotation to remove itself within 8 frames of the next caption).
- Floating labels (grammar §11 #10): a label needs a leader line or arrow to its object (`hex/DNfiewopHaL`, `hex/DZE-78dC_Xr`); the leader draws on with the label.
- Stage 1 draws it written from `lands`; stage 2 adds the write-on (SKILL step 3 and 10).
- Tell: lettering that lands on a different word, a mark fully drawn with no write-on, thin uniform red ovals (postmortem K5, K6). Fix: `lands` from the sheet, write-on from the word's onset, rough stroke.

## 3. Type interleaved with the subject; echo stack

**Type interleaved with the subject.** A headline behind the subject, a numeral behind the shoulders, letters around a prop (grammar §6; 19 pieces).
- Measured: sky / text / hero / grass layers (`ee/DZuVqDpO0Um`); title behind and in front of the car (`hex/DXoOsJdiSeU`); "2008" behind the person (`ee/DaAV2R5IuSi`); "$8 Billion" behind the head (`ee/Ddn42dGOwnB`); a word behind a row of robots (`hex/DWvkgUhAqJb`); a knocked-out word so the subject shows through (`ee/DSxDRZRDKVT`).
- How: layer the word under the cut-out in the same `Camera` (z-order is DOM order: word first, subject after; `Multiplane` for depth layers). Use `HeroWord` (`src/devices`): `<HeroWord text at|word+captions edge bleed size top enter="stamp"|"slide" cutout cutoutStyle>{ground}</HeroWord>` puts the word between the ground and the cut-out PNG (bleed one edge, keep the word readable); `heroWordPose` is the pure pose for the trace; it enters on its spoken word like any lettering. The traced box is the visible part (`registration.md` 3.6). Size the image like type (rebus, `moran/DdoVHX5gfP-`).
- Tell: the numeral across a logo or the face (`hex/DPBpZK4CbYi`). Fix: bare paper or the dark part.

**Echo stack.** The same line 3-5 times, the centre line solid, the rest fading: 5 lines at 100/45/20 % opacity (`hex/DTC-mtEiT3S`); 3 lines (`hex/DT5DKMnCSPl`, `hex/DTnEO5KCawE`); a wall of 8 faded copies for "replicate" (`ee/DWYdcYwIc5K`) (grammar §7).
- When: repetition, echo, stealth. How: copies offset slightly in time so they stack rather than move in lockstep (`moran/DVRV9hiCX4F`: slide each copy a few frames), the centre line is the spoken word and the only one traced as `lettering`.

## 4. Idle life, pose swap, shape morph

**Idle life on a held image.** One small loop while text builds, on any hold of >= 1.5 s (grammar §5; `ee/Dacjr7jNsbG`).
- Measured: a hazard sign, mousetrap or sunburst idling at ~10 deg/s (`ee/Dacjr7jNsbG`); a painting swinging ~15 deg and settling in 4 frames (`ee/Dd08TpPONpx`); props swaying on a cable (`ee/DWYdcYwIc5K`); a bobbing floating island (`ee/DcszHOXuFU6`); a slow-rotating sunburst (`hex/DWlRxTpAkLW`).
- How: seeded jitter, never `Math.random`: `wiggle` and `random(seed)` from `remotion`, 2-4 px on threes (8 Hz, craft §2.3) or Moran's snap of 6 px at 2 moves/s (`moran/DQsNA9RCcs_`, seeded by the floor of the stepped frame, not by the raw frame); edge boil on 12 fps (`stepFrame` as the seed of `Torn`/`tornClip`) for torn paper. One primary mover at a time: the hero is loud, the rest drifts under ~0.5 % per second `~`. With the camera's drift this is what keeps a 4 s window from freezing (M1).

**Pose-swap puppet.** Figures change pose by swapping drawings, not by warping (grammar §5; `Puppet` + `POSES`/`interpolatePose` for a rigged cut-out, parts and pivots in `puppet.json`): limbs re-posed every 0.5 s (`pin/350166`); a 4-pose typing loop (`pin/349999`); a 2-pose walk (`ee/DafS1lPu18x`); a walk loop is fine for <= 1.5 s (`ee/Dc0KeKyOQZE`). How: the archivist supplies the variants; swap at a frame boundary on the film's clock, same anchor, same traced id. No squash, stretch or morph of a cut-out; no turning away from the camera without a new piece (craft §2.3).

**Shape morph.** A vector shape becomes the next shape (grammar §5): a progress bar becomes a ribbon becomes the next word, 6 steps in 3 s (`hex/DV36JADibx8`); sun rays become a clock (`ee/DaAV2R5IuSi`); two dots carry a whole film with one 18 s shot (`hex/DW_DhQ6AmZ1`). How: interpolate paths with the same point count (`@remotion/paths` `interpolatePath`) as a pure function of the frame, on `stepFrame` if the film is stepped. Only for drawn shapes, never for a paper cut-out.

## 5. Data that performs

The number is shown as a quantity (grammar §5; 19 pieces).
- Measured: bars made of the product itself (`ee/DbagmfuIKyH`); two bars with cars riding on top, 5 events in 2.5 s (`hex/DVL9nizj2HD`); a line chart drawing in ~2.5 s (`hex/DW0u-HTguVA`); a ring 66 -> 80 % (`ee/DU-zVUXCD-r`); countries tinted one at a time (`hex/Dc-vZs9CkUT`); one map, 5 beats, only overlays change (`hex/DaaSLUAidHC`); a dot grid filling red over ~2 s (`pin/350010`).
- When: a claim that carries a number. The number itself steps (`devices-rhythm-camera.md` 1, count in steps); the quantity underneath it performs.
- How: bars scale from a baseline with `spring` from `remotion`; a line draws with `DrawPath` (or a stroke-dash progress from `useDraw`); `Timeline` and `Tree` lay out dated or hierarchical data, their cards arrive through `SetDown`; a grid fills at a fixed cadence; a region tints on the word that names it (the tint is the art director's colour, you time it). The digits step as in `devices-rhythm-camera.md` 1 (count in steps); `RouteMap` for a journey. Maps: desaturate the base and colour the regions in Overlay (`moran/DcTljpCgNNJ`); maps steer attention (`moran/DLShlJxRXZe`). Real captured pages treated as evidence (`moran/DLQw7mqJzRp`).
- Tell: a number said and shown as text alone while the sheet gives an object for it. Fix: the object or the graphic, then the count.

## 6. Tint wash timing

The wash on the story object at the moment the line names it is the art director's device (grammar §5); you land it: red 40 % on "something is wrong" (`hex/Da9wmeYi1ZG`); map tint on the named region (`hex/Dbc-vIXCCqe`); a prop recoloured on the money line (`ee/DbVMvRvMboX`). It starts on the word's onset, as a ramp of a few frames (an `interpolate` of the frame, no component), not a fade of the whole frame.

Gradient-map accent (S8): the named cut-out runs through luminance → dark olive → accent (`feComponentTransfer` tables ≈ 0.24/0.72/0.98 R, 0.24/0.71/0.97 G, 0.04/0.12/0.33 B for #FAF755), so its hatching survives as dark lines and its lights go full accent; a black-anchored map (0.13 at the shadows) reads muddy olive. Per-object moves: a row of figures tints one by one from the centre out (clip each figure's slice of a tinted copy); straps and shackles wipe on with a moving clip over a mask of the object; a face gets a growing disc of tint, the face still visible inside. Mask the object, never a rectangle around it (a rectangle reads as a yellow card).
