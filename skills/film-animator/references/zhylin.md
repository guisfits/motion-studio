# Zhylin: Vox lessons for the animator

Source: Denys Zhylin, tutorial notes in tutorials/zhylin-five-vox-tricks.md (timestamps as given).

1. Constant motion: nothing sits unchanged for more than a few seconds; "something is always changing" (26-33.4 s). Even the longest hold carries a slow ~1 s push-in (28-33.4 s).
2. Title reveals build word by word, ~0.5 s per word, one word per beat (4-5.5 s, 15-18 s, 47.5-50.5 s).
3. Transitions carry one scene into the next on a shared shape: zoom, slide, match cut, ring wipe (40-45 s); the outgoing and incoming frames share a shape, position or direction.
4. Numbers tick up as a counter over a few values (1989, 2002, 2015, 24.5-26 s) rather than swap.
5. Explanatory devices are drawn on, not placed: an arrow drawn between two items, a page zoomed to the highlighted line (22.5-24.5 s, 28-33.4 s).
6. Cut rate reference for a montage: ~30 distinct shots in 62.5 s, average ~1.7 s, bursts faster under list sentences (Measurements). `NORMS.frames.deadWindowS` still bounds the holds.
