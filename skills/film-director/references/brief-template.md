# `crew/brief.md` template

One page. The client reads the top block in two minutes and approves or changes it. The pace plan
and crew notes below the line are the director's decisions; the client does not vote on them.

```markdown
# Brief: <film slug>

**Question the film answers:** <one sentence the viewer could repeat after watching>
**Length / format:** <seconds, inside `NORMS.film`> · <9:16 | 16:9 | 1:1> · <platform>
**Audience:** <who, what they already know> (from the overlay)
**Tone:** <two or three words, e.g. "lively, curious, grave at the turn">

## Beats (one idea each)

| # | The viewer must grasp | Act |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | <hook: the surprising fact or question, carried by the opening line> | open |
| 2 | <stakes: why it matters> | open |
| … | <evidence, one per beat> | middle |
| n-1 | <the turn: what changes the reading> | turn |
| n | <kicker that reframes the open> | close |

**Through-line object:** <one physical thing that travels across beats, or "none">
**Reveal shot:** <the one image the film builds to>
**Promised counts:** <"three reasons" → 3 chapters, 3 cards; or "none">
**References:** <client's references; what to take from each, in one line>
**Facts to verify:** <every claim the client supplied; the writer checks each one>

---

## Pace plan (director; crew reads, client does not vote)

**Register:** A (narrated 9:16 collage explainer) <or B / C + one line why, and which R codes are waived>
**Rhythm target:** `NORMS.rhythm` (R1–R4). Opening at body rate; hook in the line and its picture.

| Device | Where (brief beat #) | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Hold on the point | <beat #, the number / conclusion / document held> | <what the viewer must read> |
| Burst before it | <beat # or "none"> | <the list or montage that resolves into the hold> |
| Breath card | <beat #s, one every 10–20 s> | <date, negation, no-picture statement, before the answer> |
| Palette flip | <beat # of the turn, direction e.g. warm → grey> | <the structural turn> |
| Dissolve | <beat # or "none"> | <time jump / chapter turn / sombre close / payoff> |
| One word per card | <the line, or "none"> | <deliberate delivery, tagline> |
| Ending | <bookend on <device> / quiet card / pull-out onto the whole> | last 5 s ≤ `NORMS.rhythm.endSlowdown` of body rate |

Crew notes: overlay parts handed to each role; deadlines; budget for paid steps.
```

Rules:

1. One idea per beat. A row with "and" in it is two beats.
2. The hook is a fact or a question in the line, never a greeting or a title card; it is not made by cutting faster (grammar §10.5).
3. The kicker answers the opening question in a way the open did not predict.
4. A client-supplied number goes in "Facts to verify", never into a beat as truth.
5. Every hold, breath card, flip and dissolve has a "why"; a row without one is deleted (`references/pace-playbook.md`).
6. No palette, type or music here: the art director and sound decide them inside the overlay. The pace plan says where the turn is, not its colours.
