# Worked example: a pace plan and a fix round

Project-agnostic. Topic: "Why did the shipping container make the world smaller?", 50 s, 9:16,
register A. Numbers in brackets are what the plan expects against grammar §10; the gates measure the
real film.

## 1. Beats and pace plan (the part of `crew/brief.md` below the line)

| # | The viewer must grasp | Act |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | In 1956 loading one ship took a week | open (year-first hook, grammar §1 "Opening-line formula") |
| 2 | Dockers carried every sack and barrel by hand | open |
| 3 | A trucker asked: why not lift the whole truck box? | middle |
| 4 | The first box ship sailed with 58 boxes | middle |
| 5 | Loading cost fell from dollars to cents per ton | middle (the point) |
| 6 | Ports that refused the box emptied | turn |
| 7 | Today almost everything you own rode in one | close (kicker) |

**Register:** A. **Rhythm target:** `NORMS.rhythm`. At ~3 words/s a 50 s narration is ~150 words;
at ~3.5 words per event that is ~43 events, of which ~1 in 5 keeps the picture (caption change only),
so the storyboard should land near 30–35 picture rows (`~`, grammar §10 rows "Spoken words per edit
event", "New picture every").

| Device | Where | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Burst | beat 2: sack, barrel, crate, bale, one row each, word-speed | a list that resolves into one idea (grammar §1, `ee/DdI5hwFuoI6`) |
| Hold on the point | beat 5: the cost figure on its document, ~3 s | the conclusion the film is remembered by (`hex/DUiOWpiCare`) |
| Hold on the point | beat 4: the ship with its 58 boxes, ~2.5 s | the evidence |
| Breath card | after beat 1 ("It took a week."), before beat 5 ("Then the price.") | after the hook; right before the answer (grammar §1 "Paper breath card") |
| Palette flip | beat 6: warm → grey | the turn: the cost of the change |
| Dissolve | beat 7 "Today" | time jump (`hex/DcF_qVxqsaA`) |
| One word per card | none | — |
| Ending | pull-out from one box to the stacked port, last card held ~2 s | decelerating ending; bookend on the box of beat 3 |

Everything else takes the overlay's default join (absent one, a carried transition).

## 2. Reading the sheet against the plan (step 6)

- Rows 4–7 are word-speed (0.3–0.5 s each) and row 8 is `device: "hold"`: the burst resolves. ✔
- Two `device: "breath-card"` rows sit where the plan put them. ✔
- Row 27 has `join: "dissolve"` on "Today"; no other row has a join. ✔
- Rows 29–31 (last 5 s): three rows, the last 2.2 s long, `zoom.kind: "pull-out"`. ✔
- Rows 12–18 (beat 4): seven rows for 26 words, one per clause. ✔ Rows 19–20 (beat 5): two rows
  for 22 words → a picture every 11 words, outside "new picture every" (2.7–8.3 words). Sent back:
  the storyboard splits beat 5 by its nouns (the old tariff sheet, the new one, the figure) before
  the art director starts.

## 3. A fix round after the draft

`crew/checks-draft.md` reports R2 🔴 (events per 10 s below range over 31–41 s) and R4 🔴 (last 5 s
at the body rate). The reviewer adds 🟠 "turn reads as the same world" at 36 s.

| Defect | Ruling | Routed to |
|---|---|---|
| R2 at 31–41 s | the sheet has the rows; the animator held one caption chunk across three clauses | animator: chunk the caption per clause (caption as the motion) |
| R4 | the animator added a flash train in the last 4 s that the plan never asked for | animator: remove it; the plan's ending stands |
| 🟠 turn at 36 s | the style bible has no grey ground for the turn | art director: the flipped ground; then animator re-renders |

Round 1 re-runs from `--stage draft` (the earliest stage touched), then a fresh reviewer. If round
2 still shows red, the client gets the open defects, ranked, with the director's recommendation.
