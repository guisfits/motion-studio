# Visual language: grounds, cut-outs, type, annotations, evidence, colour, composition

Legend: **[S]** sourced · **[M]** measured in the 28-reel reference studies (`inspirations/`) · **[I]** inference.

## 1. Grounds and textures

**Rule: never a flat solid, never a gradient, never pure black.** [M chrismoran-editorial states
it; @chrismoran__ 12/12 and earn.edits A 5/5 obey it]

| Layer (bottom → top) | What | Settings that pros state |
|---|---|---|
| Base | a real scan: newspaper page, kraft/paper, cardboard, chalkboard, cloth, wood, museum wall; one ground per idea | scan your own old newspapers / vintage paperwork / cardboard [S collage guides]; grounds change per idea, flat/grainy, never a gradient [M] |
| Grade | lifted blacks (dark grey, not black), whites pushed slightly, low contrast | [M chrismoran-editorial]; a matte shadow floor + reduced saturation + light grain, applied as one grade over everything so pieces look printed together [S, general practice] |
| Tint | one colour wash over the whole frame (warm cream, teal, maroon) | tint the paper so all pieces share a cast; this is the first of three collage tips [M Dc1ShYsplyF] |
| Texture | paper fibre / photocopy noise / 16 mm grain, blend mode chosen by ground | white ground → Darken/Multiply; black ground → Lighten/Screen; Overlay needs the texture normalised (levels) first [M DbBSCjjgbTw]; 16 mm grain Overlay at 35 % [S NoFilmSchool Vox map]; texture scaled to the object, "quarter trick": tile and remove a quarter so the scale reads real [M DcBhNhOg5kR] |
| Motion in texture | the ground breathes | 5–6 textures cycling 2–3 per second, duplicated to ~10 s, flipped/rotated for variety [S PremiumBeat]; or posterised wiggle of a paper loop at 4–8 fps [S Motion Array / Lucke] |
| Lens | chromatic aberration + 3.5 px blur at the edges only | adjustment layer, RGB split rotated slightly, Gaussian 3.5, circular mask subtracted, feather 50, for photos and clippings, "occasional not constant" [S PremiumBeat] |
| Halftone | CMYK dot pattern on photos and big type | slight blur of the dots + ink bleed + off-white paper is what reads as print [S]; fragmenting photos into halftone bands is tip 2 of 3 for collage [M Dc1ShYsplyF] |

Texture variance is tip 3: not every piece gets the same texture; mix paper, halftone, torn plate,
tape [M Dc1ShYsplyF]. A "stolen" texture workflow: scan a 70s magazine page → high-pass → overlay +
levels → chromatic aberration; the tiny imperfections are the point [M DbJSUGZpvzL].

## 2. Cut-outs

- **Edge**: a visible cut edge; either a thin white halo (paper border) on light grounds [M
  chrismoran-editorial] or a slightly rough matte; never a feathered Photoshop fade.
- **Shadow**: every cut-out casts a soft shadow onto the ground: one light angle for the whole
  scene; offset and blur grow together with virtual height; tinted low-opacity shadow rather than
  black; shadow follows the silhouette [S drop-shadow guides; I for the scaling rule].
- **Layering**: subject in front of the giant word; small satellites (birds, coins, a gavel) around
  it; objects bleed off the frame edge [M chrismoran-editorial, eb2]. Cut-outs "break outside the
  border, sit in front of the text and anchor a collage" [S brand guide].
- **Scale**: one hero cut-out per beat, big; satellites much smaller; no two objects of equal
  weight competing [M human-academy "one object, one word"].
- **Recurring figure**: the same cut-out design reused across scenes so the viewer tracks the
  character [S Korpi third-party; M Zhylin Trump cut-out].
- **Treatment**: duotone or a colour overlay (grey cut-out with pink art, pink disc behind a
  subject) ties the photo to the palette [M Zhylin 20–22 s; miraclestudios red disc].
- **Fragmenting**: slice a bust in bands, photocopy a face, halftone one half of a photo and keep
  the other clean [M human-academy; S halftone prompt guides].

## 3. Typography

Two tiers, never merged [M all studies]:

| Tier | Size / face | Words | Position | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Hero word** | huge serif / condensed / slab, often italic key word, cropped by the frame edge (SKILLS, TEXTURE, TACTILE) | 1–3 | behind or beside the subject, on the object not on the caption line | 3–8 s |
| **Caption** | small bold sans, white, caps or mixed; dark rounded box or translucent plate | 2–4 (avg 2.4) | one fixed lower spot, never moves; measured ~82 % height (chrismoran), but on 9:16 keep it above the bottom UI zone (box centre ≈ y 1380–1460 of 1920) | word-synced, 1–4 words per chunk |
| **Lead + key** | small lead word, big key word in the accent ("it's about / YOU") | 1 + 1 | on the object | per beat |
| **Label plates** | tape plate, serif label, number chip (1-2-3) | 1–2 | pinned to the object | with the object |

Rules: never a full sentence on screen [M]; never two text blocks over the proof [M]; one
hero word per scene; key word in the accent only when it is the point [M]. Zhylin's chapter card:
grey paper, big black serif numeral, small yellow dot, trick name building word by word at 0.5 s
intervals [M].

**Caption norms (standards + measured)**:
- DCMP: ≤ 2 lines preferred; min 40 frames (1 s 10 f), max 6 s per caption; sans serif medium
  weight, white, mixed case, drop/rim shadow, translucent box preferred on light grounds; bottom
  placement, move to top if it covers a face or essential graphic; break lines at natural pauses,
  never split a modifier from its noun or a name from its title [S DCMP].
- Reading rate: DCMP 130–160 wpm by audience level; BBC 160–180 wpm (~0.33–0.375 s per word,
  i.e. 1.2 s for a 4-word caption) [S]. Word-synced 2–4-word chunks at 2.5 words/s satisfy both.
- Safe zone (Reels/Shorts, 1080×1920): third-party guides converge on a central 1080×1350 (4:5)
  block for anything essential; keep ~250 px top, 280–420 px bottom and ~120 px right clear of the
  platform UI [S, unofficial]. Engine default: top 220, bottom 400, right rail 140 from y 1100
  (the phone sheet shades them). Captions sit inside the safe block, never in the bottom UI band.
- Why captions at all: 69 % watch muted in public, 25 % even in private (Verizon/Publicis survey of
  5,616 adults); 80 % say captions make them more likely to finish; 80 % of caption users are not
  hearing-impaired [S]. In every reference reel the caption is burned in [M].

## 4. Annotations (the marks)

One per frame. The vocabulary is small and hand-made [M chrismoran 12/12; Zhylin]:

| Mark | Use | Craft |
|---|---|---|
| Red arrow (hand-drawn, one stroke) | "here" on a photo/document in the hook | drawn on in 0.3–0.5 s, stays for the beat |
| Circle / scribble | isolate a detail on a painting or page | one stroke, slightly imperfect |
| Underline swipe | the key phrase on a document | left→right, 0.4 s; colour = accent |
| Highlighter | a line in a bill or article | opaque yellow at ~40–60 %, follows the text line [I] |
| Redaction bar | what is hidden / removed | black bar, stepped reveal [I] |
| Number tick / counter | a figure growing | tick SFX optional, ends on the number held |
| Sticker (thumb, tape, "THIS IS BAD" plate) | commentary | sparingly, one per scene |
| White ring wipe | transition | only as a transition, not a mark |

The annotation always points at evidence that is readable (hold ≥ 2.5 s after the mark lands) [M].

## 5. Archival documents as evidence

- Show the actual page: newspaper scan, bill, PhD dissertation, 1914 medical article, tweet,
  Threads post; crop to the paragraph, then push in and highlight the sentence [S Edwards; M Zhylin
  28–33 s].
- Match cut on text: ≥ 12 images where the same phrase appears, each repositioned so the phrase is
  centred, first clips ~8 frames then shortening, camera-click SFX on each cut [S Motion Array,
  Harris style].
- Page turn that feels real: fold radius + posterise to 8 fps [M Dafr0KjAkve].
- Vintage TV / film-window crop for footage; scan lines + solid + travelling shape + colour cast
  [M DaQeqljgc7y, Dcm63HBAHzT].
- Gate weave: two small wiggles (position + flicker) on a still photo make it read as projected film;
  remove it and the photo "looks dead" [M Da3YpRipHuF].
- Credibility: name the source of each figure, label estimates as estimates [S Korpi third-party;
  Edwards cites sources in descriptions]; Vox has a colleague checking every map [S Sendaydiego].

## 6. Maps and data

- Maps: desaturated base, one highlighted region or route, labels in the film's serif, textures and
  clouds on top, camera storyboarded before animating (close on Paris → slow pull-out) [S
  NoFilmSchool; M DcTljpCgNNJ 5 steps: clean map, shapes, overlay, burn reveal, texture + clouds].
  Google Earth Studio for terrain fly-overs with camera moves "that weren't too crazy" [S Fong].
- Data: one number per scene; a bar chart with one highlighted bar beats a table [S third-party];
  physical metaphors (a paper clock turned into a pie chart) [S Sendaydiego]; print the chart on
  paper and walk through it on camera when the data is the story [S Fong vaccine video].
- Counters: numbers tick up and land; disc counters (1989 → 2002 → 2015) one per beat [M Zhylin].

## 7. Colour discipline

- Palette = neutrals from the paper + **one accent** (chrismoran: pink / teal / red / olive, one per
  reel) or **two** (earn.edits: yellow + red; red + cream) [M]. One accent mark per frame.
- Grade unifies: a single tint and lifted blacks over every element so photos, scans and type look
  printed on the same stock [M chrismoran; S].
- Avoid the "grey and neon" tech look; Abram uses dark browns, greens, blues [S].
- Dark grounds: an old page or a place photo graded dark (blacks lifted, never pure black), a
  warm tint and grain; the single accent stays the only saturated colour [M collage-reels-trio].

## 8. Composition

- ≤ 3 elements: hero, caption, one annotation [M].
- Hero centred or on a third, satellites orbit, giant word cropped at the bottom or side edge
  [M miraclestudios, chrismoran].
- Swiss micro-typography in corners stays quiet (tiny labels) [M miraclestudios].
- Every frame has a subject; no dead frames between beats [M earn.edits].
- Vertical: design to the 1080×1350 centre; let grounds and cropped words bleed into the UI bands,
  never the proof or the caption [S safe zones, unofficial; M].
- Real product/UI, when it is the proof, is a first-class shot: large (≥ 50 % width), held
  1.5–4 s, header never covered by a caption [M chrismoran screen recordings 40–50 % of runtime].
