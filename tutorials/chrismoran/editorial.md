# Chris Moran: "the editorial feel is the background" (42 s tutorial)

Moved from `inspirations/vox-collage/collage-reels-trio.md`: a tutorial, so knowledge, not inspiration.

<https://www.instagram.com/reel/DY7TSLJAQef/>

- The trick, said in the reel itself: **never a flat solid background**. Layer real scans
  (newspaper pages, political architecture) behind the subject, set the blacks to a **dark grey,
  not pure black**, push the whites slightly, add a **tint** on top, then a little **texture**.
  Contrast stays subtle.
- Huge serif word with the subject cut-out **in front of it**; birds/small cut-outs flying.
- One hand-drawn **red arrow** pointing at the thing ("See it?").
- Subtitles: small caps in a dark box at the lower third, one phrase per beat.
- Collage of symbolic objects around a building (gavel, scales, a hand from the clouds), a
  white halo around cut-outs on a light ground.
