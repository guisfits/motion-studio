# Tutorials

Knowledge sources, not inspiration. Each entry is a teacher explaining *how* to make the
editorial-collage (Vox-style) look: editing, camera, texture, type, sound, direction. Most of each
video is the teacher talking, so the frames say little about the look; the value is the technique.
The crew skills (`skills/film-*`) turn these lessons into numbered rules
(`skills/_shared/moran.md` for director, art director and animator; `skills/film-<role>/references/moran.md` for the other roles), each tagged with its source here.

Finished pieces to look at for grammar live in `inspirations/` instead (`vox-collage/` for the target look, `motion-graphics/` for the other register).

| Teacher | Notes | What it teaches |

| Denys Zhylin (`denyszhylin`), motion designer / video essayist | `zhylin-five-vox-tricks.md` | how Vox explains: chapter cards, montage under lists, second-by-second structure |
| Vox's own video team (Joss Fong, Joe Posner, Estelle Caswell and others, in their words) | `vox-design-practice.md` | how the practice works: motif test, formats, source stack, review stages; one trailer watched |

Each "Build:" line in the Moran notes says how the After Effects technique maps to this engine's
HTML/canvas/SVG + ffmpeg stack. His free resource files (textures, overlays, presets) are kept by
each project that uses them, as material.

**Local cache, never committed.** Each teacher's downloaded reels, whisper transcripts and frames
live in `tutorials/<teacher>/cache/` (git-ignored: the reels and their words are the teacher's).
What is committed is our paraphrase: the notes and the lessons ledger.
