# claude-pop-donald (studied 2026-10-08)

Source: <https://x.com/donaldjewkes/status/2102801274173587569> · 141.6 s, 16:9.

## Grammar observed

- One fixed limited palette for 140 s (risograph: orange, pink, navy, cream) with halftone and
  paper grain: every scene is different, the palette never changes, so it reads as one film.
- A new composition every ~3 s; recurring motifs bind them: radial sunbursts, a docket/label in
  the corner (date, counter), sticker captions.
- Lyrics as typography with two registers: huge blocky words that fill the frame on the hook
  and small subtitle stickers elsewhere.
- A recurring character as the thread through every scene.
- Bookend: opens on a small drawn flower with a "time horizon" label, ends on a hand drawing
  the same flower in pencil with the label's earlier value: the end explains the start.
- Generate-then-trace: base motion from a video model, every frame redrawn in code on top.

## General takeaways

- Hold one palette strictly across many compositions; variety comes from composition, not colour.
- Bind scenes with one recurring motif and one recurring label (a docket with a reference or a year).
- Two type registers: huge on the climax word, small elsewhere.
- Bookend: open and close on the same object, changed by what the film told.

## Never take

The character, the anime style, the palette, the lyrics.
