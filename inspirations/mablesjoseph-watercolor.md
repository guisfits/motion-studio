## Grammar observed

- Painterly rendering in code: watercolour washes, soft bleeding edges, paper texture, no hard lines.
- A colour script by act: blue night room → warm sunset street → lavender dusk → dark night
  with stars → pink dawn. The colour change is the act change.
- Light is the storytelling device: a glowing lantern companion, sparkles, a sky filling with
  lights at the climax.
- Bookend: opens on a single glowing dot on dark, closes on the same dot.
- Slow pacing, long holds, wide shots with a small character; close-ups only at emotional beats.

## General takeaways

- A colour script per chapter, using the film's own theme tokens (night → day → warm → night).
- Light as meaning: a source that grows when the idea grows.
- Painterly washes as a texture layer under paper grounds, not as whole scenes.
- Bookend on one point of light.
