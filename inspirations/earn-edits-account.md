## Sound (all 12)

- **Integrated loudness is the same on every reel: -14.1 to -15.0 LUFS (A: -14.1 to -14.3), LRA 1.1-2.6 LU** (these are Instagram-normalised exports; treat -14 as the target, LRA ≤ 3). 1 s RMS in A reels has p10-p90 spread of only 3-5 dB (e.g. yur -16/-13/-12 dBFS). No spikes above the voice level.
- **Voice is on from frame 0 to the end** in every reel. No silence > 0.4 s at -35 dB in 11/12 reels (only ejc has one). Music/ambience is a continuous bed under it, never in front of it. Whisper-turbo transcribed every A reel without a single wrong word, including through the busiest stretches, so SFX/music never masked speech.
- **SFX density cannot be isolated from music + speech by ffmpeg** (honest limit). Proxies: spectral-flux onsets are 10-14 per 10 s in A (includes syllables), low-band impact/whoosh hits are 4.8-11.7 per 10 s (yur 11.7, d08 7.4, z4d 6.4, eg9 4.8, eb2 4.8). Reading the 1 s RMS and the way the track sits flat at one level, the SFX are short, low in level and tied to the cuts, not a layer of their own. There is no racket of effects.
- **Ending:** the CTA line is spoken at normal level; the music tail drops 3-5 dB on the last second (yur -19, d08 -21 in the last second), no stinger. No extended outro: the CTA _is_ the outro, 4.9-5.8 s, ends on the URL.

## What makes these good (norms, weighted to genre A)

1. **Length 27-47 s (A median 36 s)**, one topic, one thesis. A 15-25 s film has no room to reason.
2. **Voice rate 2.25-3.0 words/s (A median 2.5 ≈ 150 wpm), 80-106 words per film**, one sentence of 2.5-4.5 s per visual idea. Voice starts on frame 0 with a complete sentence.
3. **Hook = a surprising fact, a number, or a "What if" question, said whole in the first 2-4.5 s** (A: all five, 1.9-4.7 s long). The image under it is literal and iconic (year scroll, face, hospital, painter), not abstract.
4. **Argument shape in A:** hook → context/why → turn ("But it gets crazier", "Why?", "And here's a strange fact") at ~50% → payoff sentence → question or verdict. Always one explicit turn word.
5. **A new visual beat every 2-4 s** (detector ASL 2.0-5.3 s; eg9 4.6, d08 5.3; eb2 densest at 2.5). Never faster than ~2 s except for a list inside one sentence (eb2 creams / serums / treatments, ~1 s each, said as three words).
6. **Hold ≥ 5 s on the hero object when it is the point** (d08 holds the painting 8+ s, longest shot 17.8 s with small moves; eg9 10 s; yur 13 s on the arguments). Camera stays alive with slow push/tilt, not static.
7. **On-screen text:** the bottom subtitle is **2-4 words, word-synced, white with a dark pad**, always the same spot. The big title is **1-3 words** chunky slab/serif, one per beat, in one accent (yellow or red), placed on the object not on the caption line. Never a full sentence on screen.
8. **One number or one name per beat, huge** (381, 80%, 230%, 1930, 1892, "13 TEAMS").
9. **One ground per idea, a different ground for the next idea** (desk, chalkboard, wall, sky); the scene change = the idea change, and each scene has 1 cut-out + 1 title + the caption.
10. **Palette ≤ 2 accents per reel** (z4d yellow+red; eb2 red+cream; yur yellow+red+blue ground; d08 orange+cream), neutrals do the rest.
11. **Sound:** -14 LUFS, LRA ≤ 3, voice continuous, music bed flat under it, SFX short and on the cut, zero SFX stacked on a spoken emphasis. 1 s RMS never leaves a 5 dB window.
12. **Close = calm question or verdict, then a pivot word.** eg9 sets its last idea ("Coincidence, or the ultimate crime?") alone on a plain ground for ~2.5 s before the CTA; the others say the closing sentence and go straight to "Editors, ...".
13. **CTA = one spoken line of ≤ 11 words and ≤ 4.5 s, shown on a real product visual (phone, hands holding file tiles) with the URL as text; the whole outro 4.9-5.8 s = 12-18% of the film.** The pivot is a single audience word ("Editors,") that makes the product feel like the answer to the topic.
14. **No hard cuts into black, no dead frames:** every frame has a subject; transitions are cheap (whip, object slide, ground swap), same few per film.
15. Real-product look is minimal in A: the app/product appears only in the last 5 s.

## Takeaways for the Vox critic (checkable rules)

- Film length ≥ 27 s (target 45-60 s for a narrated Reel); word count / duration between 2.2 and 3.0 wps.
- First sentence complete by 4.5 s and made of a fact, number or "What if".
- A "turn" sentence between 40 and 60% of the runtime.
- Subtitle ≤ 4 words per chunk; big title ≤ 3 words; never two text blocks over the proof or the product UI.
- Median beat length ≥ 2.5 s; no beat < 1.5 s except an explicit 3-item list.
- Hero object hold ≥ 5 s at least twice.
- ≤ 2 accent colours in a frame; ≤ 1 accent mark per beat.
- Integrated -14 LUFS (±1), LRA ≤ 3, 1 s RMS window ≤ 5 dB, voice from frame 0, music bed continuous, SFX on cuts only.
- Outro 4.5-6 s, one spoken line ≤ 11 words, product visual + URL, preceded by a calm closing sentence/question.
