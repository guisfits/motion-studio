# Three editorial-collage reels (studied 2026-10-09, frames at 1 fps)

Three reels sent as references for editorial collage. What each teaches, as grammar:

## chrismoran-editorial (42 s): "the editorial feel is the background"

<https://www.instagram.com/reel/DY7TSLJAQef/>

- The trick, said in the reel itself: **never a flat solid background**. Layer real scans
  (newspaper pages, political architecture) behind the subject, set the blacks to a **dark grey,
  not pure black**, push the whites slightly, add a **tint** on top, then a little **texture**.
  Contrast stays subtle.
- Huge serif word with the subject cut-out **in front of it**; birds/small cut-outs flying.
- One hand-drawn **red arrow** pointing at the thing ("See it?").
- Subtitles: small caps in a dark box at the lower third, one phrase per beat.
- Collage of symbolic objects around a building (gavel, scales, a hand from the clouds), a
  white halo around cut-outs on a light ground.

## human-academy-vox-bust (76 s): "one object, one word"

<https://www.instagram.com/reel/DbTxeqsBWST/>

- Opens on a classical painting (an orator before a crowd) with **one key word in the accent
  colour** over it; a hand-drawn red circle on the painting.
- Kinetic captions: a small lead-in word + one big key word per beat, the key word in accent.
- **One cut-out object alone** on a calm light-grey paper ground per beat (an eye, a stack of
  money, a bust, a face): minimal, centred, a soft shadow.
- The bust sliced in bands, each band from a different source; brief chromatic blur on transitions.

## miraclestudios-boulder (15 s): "a new image every second"

<https://www.instagram.com/reel/DamzkLfAFYy/>

- Pace: a new subject every ~1 s, alternating light grey and black grounds (a 15 s poster
  piece, not a narrated argument: this pace does not transfer to a 45–60 s explainer).
- **Giant word cropped at the bottom edge**, behind the subject.
- A big **red disc** behind the cut-out subject as the only accent; dotted orbit lines.
- Small lead word + big key word ("it's about / **you**").
- Swiss micro-typography in the corners (tiny labels), kept quiet.

## General takeaways

| Their move | As a reusable rule |
|---|---|
| layered scans, dark grey not black, tint, texture | dark grounds are graded scans: lifted blacks, one tint, grain |
| subject in front of a huge serif word | hero word behind the cut-out (depth by overlap) |
| giant word cropped at the edge | hero word bleeds off one edge; crop so it never reads as another word |
| red disc / red arrow | one accent shape or one hand mark per frame, nothing else saturated |
| lead word + key word captions | small lead + big key word; key word in the accent only when it is the point |
| one object per beat on calm paper | one hero object per beat; the hook may move faster than the body |
