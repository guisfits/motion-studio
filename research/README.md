# Research behind the film crew

Evidence the `skills/film-*` rules cite. The skills quote these files by path; keep the paths stable.

| File | What it is |
|------|------------|
| `research/craft.md` | Craft research for a paper cut-out collage Reel (9:16, 60-90 s): numbers-first rules for camera, timing, piece motion, captions, sync, pacing, sound, endings. Numbered sources `[n]`, sections `2.1`-`2.10`, a list of tells in section 3. Tags: `[own]` is judgment to tune, not fact |
| `research/crew-roles.md` | Why the crew is split into these roles: studies of multi-agent film pipelines, the role-by-role keep/add/drop table, the order of work, and the failure modes each extra hand-off adds |
| `research/external-skills.md` | What four generator-driven video skills do well and badly (script shape, locked look, approval gates, audio recipe), mapped to what the crew takes, adapts or refuses. Source folders are named in its header (`pynk`, `adv/vox`, `adv/coll`, `adv/expl`, `muapi`, `mega`) |
| `research/editing-grammar.md` | The measured editing and composition grammar of 323 finished collage pieces: registers (§0), 78 devices by area (§1–§9), the register-A rhythm model (§10), odd-looking tells ranked (§11), where the evidence contradicts Moran (§12), the role map (§13). Evidence ids `hex/…`, `ee/…`, `pin/…` point into `inspirations/vox-collage/collage-study/findings.jsonl` |
| `research/sound-grammar.md` | The measured sound of 201 of those pieces: loudness, bed level and swell, cue density and placement, silences; the evidence behind `NORMS.audio` |
| `research/first-film-postmortem.md` | The defect list of a failed first cut, ids C1-C7 (coarse) and K1-K10 (craft), with the norm each one breaks. The skills cite these ids |

How to cite: `research/craft.md 2.7` (file and section), `research/first-film-postmortem.md C3` (file and id),
`research/external-skills.md` (file, optionally the source folder).
